Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pelicano's Pizza known for in Rocky River, OH?

Pelicano's Pizza is known for generous-topping pizza, great sauce and crust, broasted chicken, pizza by the slice, sheet pizza, chicken wings, white pizza, party subs, and catering across Rocky River, Lakewood, and Fairview Park.

Pizza Broasted Chicken Pizza by the Slice Sheet Pizza Wings White Pizza Catering JoJo Potatoes
How do I order from Pelicano's Pizza in Rocky River?

Call us at (440) 333-0558. We take orders by phone for pickup and delivery, seven days a week. For catering orders, please call at least 72 hours in advance.

Do you offer delivery to Lakewood and Fairview Park?

Yes — we deliver to Rocky River, Lakewood, Fairview Park, and surrounding areas. Call us at (440) 333-0558 to confirm delivery availability for your address.

What is broasted chicken and why is Pelicano's known for it?

Broasted chicken is pressure-fried chicken — crispy on the outside, incredibly juicy inside. Pelicano's serves it as a combo with JoJo potatoes or garlic mashed potatoes and coleslaw, and it's available in party sizes for catering. It's one of our most-loved menu items alongside the pizza.

Do you offer sheet pizza and catering for events?

Yes. Half sheet pizza (20 cuts) is $22 and the giant sheet (40 cuts) is $30 — both with 30-minute notice. For larger catering orders including broasted chicken, salad, and party subs, we ask for at least 72 hours notice. Call (440) 333-0558 to place your catering order.

Do you sell pizza by the slice?

Yes — pizza by the slice is available during regular store hours. Great for a quick lunch or dinner. Call ahead at (440) 333-0558 to check availability.

Where is Pelicano's Pizza located?

We're at 2218 Wooster Road, Rocky River, Ohio 44116. Parking is available on-site. We're conveniently located near Lakewood and Fairview Park.
